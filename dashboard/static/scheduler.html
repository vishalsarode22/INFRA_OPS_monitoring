Scheduler

Per-system monitoring cadence
A saved, enabled schedule runs a full sweep of that system (all T-codes, the Excel/PDF report and the e-mail), the same as Monitor now on its card. A system that was never saved shows not scheduled and never runs on its own. The Profiles page runs its own checks as well. A sweep drives SAP GUI for several minutes, so only one runs at a time: schedules are checked every 20 seconds, and one that falls due while another sweep runs starts after it. Times are this VM's clock.
